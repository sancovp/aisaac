# RULE 01 — THE IDENTITY (what this site makes deducible; read with 00-SITE-CANON)

**THE IDENTITY: Isaac builds WORLDS out of language. Everyone else builds tools.**
Tools are things a person uses; worlds are realities with laws, economies, seasons,
and inhabitants — and Isaac's tools fall out of his worlds as side effects. The corpus
is one long receipt: the one law is world-legislation (`/agent/.claude/rules/00`), the
ratchet is world-physics (`cave_teams/season.py`), the myth is a world's founding
document whose protagonist slot is per-player (`SANCTUARY-MYTH-ORIGIN.md` Part VI:
"Oliver Powers is you"), the research institute publishes as a living world-ledger,
and the viral wave everyone chased was eight unpersisted minutes of what he has years
of, receipted (`garage-lab/GAUNTLET-LOOP-ANATOMY.md`).

**THE PRIME LAW OF THIS SITE: superiority is NEVER stated. It is deduced.** No
accomplishment lists. No "X years / N repos" flexes as claims. The visitor must be
able to conclude, on their own, within the first minutes, that this person operates a
level above the field — because they EXPERIENCED it, not because they were told.

## The three deducibility mechanics (every design decision serves one)

1. **THE DOOR IS A RUNNING WORLD.** index.html opens INTO something alive — the lab
   explainer's live engines / a world render — with "Enter", never "Learn more". A
   visitor's first 8 seconds are spent INSIDE an artifact no tool-builder can ship.
2. **THE DEPTH GRADIENT.** Every artifact links one level deeper into the system that
   generated it (pattern → catalog entry → source file → running test), receipts at
   every layer. Five minutes of descent = the visitor has verified more depth than a
   competitor's whole site contains. Depth-with-receipts at corpus scale is unfakeable
   — that is the proof, and it is self-administered.
3. **THE GANGSHAR SORT.** The site grades its decoder: engineers who pull threads land
   on running code; operators land on running businesses; tourists get a world worth
   walking through. Nobody is lied to; everybody is sorted. Content that grades its
   reader IS the superiority demonstration (BATTLESPACE §7n).

## Register

Door one-liner register (plain first, lore after — never prerequisite): "I build
worlds that do work." / "This isn't a website. It's a world. You're in it now." The
exoteric flavor lane is the GG Legend (SANCTUARY-MYTH-ORIGIN Part V) ONLY after plain
statement. The visitor is addressed as the protagonist (Part VI), consent-gated: the
door invites, never traps.

## What this rule bans

Accomplishment-list heroes · "highly organized complexity" as the pitch · any sentence
of the form "I am better/ahead/the only one" · fake liveness (a world-slot ships real
or ships as a labeled placeholder — never generated numbers) · saying what can be
deduced.
