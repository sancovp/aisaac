# RULE 03 — THE FUNNEL LAW (how every page, screen and offer of this site sells)

TRIGGER:[writing or changing ANY copy, screen, funnel, page section, offer, cart or button on this site · a ruling from
*USER* about how the site sells · about to check a change to a funnel]

READ FIRST, WHOLE, BEFORE ONE WORD OF COPY:[every `hormozi-*` and `expert-secrets-*` skill in `.claude/skills/` (SKILL.md
whole) · `aida-fractal-value-ladder` (SKILL.md + reference.md) · the whole `b2b-bootcamp/` (JOKE-MEDICINE, CLINIC,
DOCTOR, SCREENING, PSA, LIFE, MEDICINE, OFFERS, SNAKE OIL, OBVIOUSNESS PARADOX, UNICORN, the cosmology, the pitch) ·
`docs/the-door.md` (the model, the avatars, the offers, the cart) · never one skill picked for the task]

DO:[
- TAKE A RULING AS CLASS LOGIC: it is an instruction at the genus level — specialize it at once to every surface it
  governs (every avatar, every screen, every funnel page), never only to the one *USER* pointed at, and never paste its
  words onto a page as copy
- HOLD THE FRAME: order the information flow so no screen names or assumes what the visitor has not yet been given —
  before the reveal, every screen speaks from where the visitor already is, in their terms; the product is first named
  at THE REVEAL
- TELL, NEVER ASK: every screen is a statement in the second person that presupposes where they are and where they are
  going; the one question on the site is the survey's "which one are you", with nothing around it; the visitor answers
  by recognising themselves in the cards
- GIVE EACH AVATAR ITS OWN CONVERSATION (CLOSER, closed as DOCTOR): opens on the choice they made → what they want →
  what is happening (they name themselves in it) → what they tried → where it ends if nothing changes (each pain carried
  to its end and left with them) → THE TRICK (the expectation, then the literal thing — JobWorld working, on screen — so
  the meaning snaps: JOKE = MEDICINE = SALE) → where they will be → their doubts answered → their offer at the cart
- BUILD EVERY OFFER AROUND JOBWORLD: JobWorld is the main product of every offer, and a bump, upsell or downsell only puts
  more on JobWorld (seats, the agency level); a B2B business alone may start with the retainer and is sold into JobWorld
  from inside; each offer is a Grand Slam built from the skills — a MAGIC name, its core, bonuses named for their benefit
  and each answering an objection the conversation labelled; nothing done-with-you is offered before payment except the
  B2B retainer
- CHECK BY READING: read every changed surface top to bottom as the visitor who lands on it cold — what does each screen
  assume, does it hold the frame, does it tell, does it land — and fix what the reading finds; layout gates
  (`tools/style_qa.py`, a phone-width look) check only layout
]

NOT:[
- name, link or label the product before the reveal (a top bar, a card, a pill, a button included)
- ask the visitor anything but which one they are; put a question in a heading
- mash avatars into one script, or run one conversation for all
- offer anything that is not JobWorld or more on JobWorld (the B2B retainer start excepted); fill an offer from an older
  price list; set a price, a guarantee or a scarcity (law 1 — they are *USER*'s)
- state anything false — no invented number, no invented scarcity, no fake case study (law 3)
- treat a mechanical check as proof that a funnel works
- quote an angry message or a swear of *USER*'s anywhere
]
