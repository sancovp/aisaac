# The comment-keyword funnel (Maria Wendt's machine, with *USER*'s additions)

**The whole machine:** a post → "comment WORD" → one automated DM: "here's the link" → the point of
conversion. Nothing else. Simplicity is the rule ("if it's not easy, I don't do it"): no 17-step DM
flows, no quiz, no email capture in front of the sale. Effort goes into VOLUME at the top, never into
optimizing a back end nobody reaches yet.

## 1. The point of conversion — choose it per offer, then send everyone straight there

| offer | where the link goes | then |
|---|---|---|
| low ticket ($27–57; impulse) | the checkout page | email the BUYERS (that list is the repeat revenue) |
| call-sold (high ticket) | the booking page | booked within 24–48 hours, closed within 24–48 hours, 1 in 3 |
| a lead form | the form | answered at once (speed to lead) |

Anything placed between interest and that point (a freebie, a nurture sequence, a webinar, a VSL) cools the
lead: interest is highest the moment they find you. Her six-month test: straight to checkout beat email-first.

## 2. The content — two buckets, every piece measured

- **PITCH** makes money today: one clear call to action and its keyword. At least one pitch every
  morning (her slot: 7 a.m. Pacific) so every day starts with sales.
- **NURTURE** builds trust: the diary — what you are going through, said vulnerably; an emotion felt in the
  body is the signal for a post. Nurture is PROOF you live the result your buyer wants.
- **ONE THING, POSTED UNTIL IT IS LOUD** (*USER*): post about one success, over and over. Every post becomes
  about it, the symbols become obvious, people get who you are and want the benefit from you — and every
  post points them at the one thing you sell. That is the five ones applied to content.
- **Measure every piece by the money it makes**, through its own keyword — 30k views and 50 sales beats
  100k views and 3. Early on, track views → followers; then views → sales.
- **The founder writes the words** (captions, posts, emails, subject lines): it is the money-making work.

## 3. Distribution — get in front of the right people before you have an audience (*USER*)

- **Comment where your buyers already are**: on the posts of famous and large accounts about your subject,
  with real substance ("my opinion on X…"); the people reading those comments see you. Ten targeted
  people a day compounds.
- **Reformat the conversation**: whatever your market is talking about, post your take on it, with the keyword.
- **Elevator ads** once anyone has paid you $1: start at $5 a day; spend what each sale returns; the rule is
  at least 2.5× back on the first purchase (AOV ≥ 2.5 × cost to acquire). A plateau means the warm market
  is spent: the cold market needs better creative, a better page and a higher order value.

## 4. Volume — the 90-day ramp, then the levels

Month 1: once a week, same time, never missed · month 2: two or three a week · month 3: up to five. **Miss
one, start from zero.** The ramp trains keeping your word to yourself, which is the identity the whole
thing rests on. After it: level 1 one reel a day · level 2 a reel, a carousel and a story a day · level 3 as
many as you can (hers: seven to the feed).

## 5. The page the link lands on

It answers two questions: **what do I get** (the result, not the modules) and **why now** (urgency — flash
sales, coupons, deadlines, and the deadline is the deadline: no exceptions, out of respect for whoever
bought on time).
