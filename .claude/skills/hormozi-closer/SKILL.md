# Hormozi CLOSER — the sales process

## Core principle

**You cannot solve someone's problem until they have said it, named it, seen why it was never solved, wanted where they are
going, lost their reasons not to, and felt good about deciding.** CLOSER is those six moves, in order. Each step consumes what
the step before produced; a step skipped leaves its output missing, and the close fails at the step that needed it.

## The six steps — what each takes in, what it must produce

| step | it takes | it produces | the move |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C — CLARIFY** | the client | their DESIRE and the PROBLEM in their words: why they are here, why they want help now | Ask why they came. Make sure you both are solving the same problem — never solve the wrong one. |
| **L — LABEL** | the problem | the problem's NAME, said by them | Name it so it stops being abstract and the solution becomes clear. They must admit the problem — you cannot solve a problem nobody owns. |
| **O — OVERVIEW** | the client + the labelled problem | their HISTORY: what they already tried, what worked, what did not | Ask what they have tried. Repeat it back so they feel heard and you prove you listened. Never prescribe something that already failed them. |
| **S — SELL THE VACATION** | their history | the RESULT, as a picture of where they will be | Show the destination, not the journey — the details sound like work. Their own picture of the result is usually blurry: make it sharp. |
| **E — EXPLAIN** | the result they now want | their CONCERNS, each met with the SOLUTION to it, and their REASSURANCE | Draw out every no and reason not to. Objections are usually real concerns; meeting each one is what makes them feel taken care of. |
| **R — REINFORCE** | the reassurance | the DECISION, and their SATISFACTION with it | Right after buying they doubt it. Confirm they chose right — and keep confirming: every later touch either reinforces the decision or erodes it. |

The chain, as one line: **client → desire + problem → label → history → result → concerns → solution → reassurance →
decision → satisfaction.**

## How to run it

1. **Clarify before anything is shown.** A flow that opens on the product skips C; the visitor never said why they came.
2. **Labelling is theirs to do.** Offer the names ("is it this?") and let them pick; a label you impose is not admitted.
3. **Overview prevents the repeat failure.** Ask "what have you tried?" and route around it — a tool they already bought and
   quit, an agency that did not deliver, a course they never finished.
4. **The vacation is the destination only.** One sentence of where they will be beats a feature list.
5. **Explain is a list, worked through.** Each concern → its answer → "does that settle it?" Do not move on with one open.
6. **Reinforce starts at the purchase and never stops.** The confirmation, the first-day experience and every later message
   are reinforcement.

## Applied to THE DOOR (iwantaiformybusiness.com; aisaac `docs/the-door.md`)

- **The avatar self-selects first; then each avatar gets ITS OWN CLOSER**, attuned to it — never one script for all, never
  avatars mashed together.
- **Every CLOSER ends at the same cart:** exactly what that avatar needs INSIDE JobWorld — the AI system they do it in
  themselves, which works with them and for them.
- **Then the hand-holding**, by who they are: the community for individuals · a B2B engagement on a retainer for businesses.
- It is not selling AI transformation. Example — the entrepreneur who wants to learn and grow: C "why are you here — to
  learn how to sell, market, run it?" → L the pain named → O what they have tried → S where they will be → E "need
  frameworks? here they are (the B2B bootcamp) — and they are inside JobWorld too" → R "want that?" → the cart.

## Never

- show the offer before Clarify · name the problem for them · skip Overview and re-sell what already failed them
- sell the journey (the steps, the setup) instead of the destination · move past an open concern · stop reinforcing after
  the sale · run one CLOSER for every avatar
