How I got here

I have worked in AI since 2023, when my first job was building agents for a film production company. I came to it from philosophy. As an undergraduate I studied ontologies — the discipline of deciding what things are and how they relate to each other — and that turned out to be the job. Before you can automate how a business works, somebody has to map what it actually is. So I kept mapping.

Since then I have built software, agents, automations, SOPs, second brains, a logic engine and a lot of ontologies. Right now I am building a Digital Twin of an Organization: one live model of how a business runs, that its people and its AI both work from.
