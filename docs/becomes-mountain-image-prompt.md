## PROMPT

```
A clean 3D render in the exact style and with the exact characters of the reference images: chunky
low-poly toy figures with big heads and simple friendly faces, soft matte plastic, small navy-blue
desks, grey laptops. Isometric three-quarter view from above, orthographic camera.

ONE LOW-POLY MOUNTAIN fills the scene, rising from the LOWER LEFT to a summit at the UPPER RIGHT: soft
white and very pale grey matte facets, like a paper model, with three wide flat LEDGES stepping up its
face. A single thin solid black line, like ink on a technical drawing, is the path: it starts at the
foot of the mountain on the far left and zig-zags up across all three ledges to the summit.

LEDGE 1, low on the left — FOUNDATION: THE OWNER from the first reference (dark skin, short black hair,
round glasses, green shirt, a small glowing gold orb floating just above his head) sitting at one navy
desk. Beside him ONE agent — the same toy body but entirely smooth matte sky-cyan with no hair and no
clothing detail — at its own small desk, one thin black line joining the two. A small plain GOLD pennant
flag (no symbol) stands on the ledge's edge.

LEDGE 2, halfway up — HARDENING: a small cluster of navy desks where worker figures (blue caps, yellow
shirts or grey suits) sit beside several cyan agents; thin black lines wire all of them together into
one clean, even web. A small plain CYAN pennant flag stands on the ledge's edge.

LEDGE 3, high on the right — CLOSURE: the whole organisation has become ONE GIANT AGENT — a single large
smooth cyan toy figure, three times the height of the others, sitting calmly with the small workers and
agents gathered around its feet, every black line now running into it. A small plain VIOLET pennant flag
stands on the ledge's edge.

THE SUMMIT — the city: on the flat top of the mountain, a small bright CITY of simple white low-poly
buildings of different heights, each with its own tiny cyan agent at its door, all the buildings wired
to each other by thin black lines into one network. THE OWNER stands at the edge of the city, relaxed,
hands in pockets, looking out over the climb, his gold orb glowing above him. The city is the only place
the gold glow repeats: a soft gold light in the windows of the tallest building.

Keep generous empty white space around the mountain and along all four edges; nothing touches an edge.
The three ledges and the summit are clearly separate, each readable at a glance.

Background: PURE WHITE (#FFFFFF), seamless, no sky, no clouds, no horizon, no ground plane beyond the
mountain's own base. Only soft light grey contact shadows. Bright even studio light. Calm, orderly,
premium, uncluttered.

Wide landscape, 16:9, at least 2400 x 1350.
```
