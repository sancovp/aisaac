## 1. THE FIRST HARD FACT — $100K is cumulative, and that is harder than it sounds

$100,000 over twelve months is **not** "reach $8,333/month." It is the **area under the
curve**, which means the run rate has to go well past $8,333 to average it.

Starting from $1,000/month, growing steadily, ending the year at $16,000/month averages
about $8,500. **So the real target is roughly $16,000/month MRR by month 12** — and
every month spent below the line has to be paid back by a month above it. **The months
lost at the start are the most expensive ones in the whole plan.**

> ⚠ **SECTIONS 2 AND 4 BELOW WERE BUILT ON A WRONG NUMBER — CORRECTED IN §7.**
> They assume 10 delivery hours per advisory client per month. Isaac's actual figure is
> **~3** (two hours of sessions plus about an hour supervising the agent while it
> compiles the options). The capacity ceiling this document reasons from is therefore
> **wrong by a factor of three**, and §7 supersedes both — kept in place rather than
> rewritten so the correction is legible.
