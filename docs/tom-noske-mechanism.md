## 3. The video written in 3⁴ NOTATION — the decomposition operator, not the DONGSUM depth (G6: the closures are real; the counts are a reading, not load-bearing)

```
VIDEO = ( ( 2 + 5·(3+1) ) +2 +1 +1 ) +1 +1

  2          promise · metaphor (atoms)
  5·(3+1)    five tests, each three atoms (question · scale · consequence pair),
             each closed +1 by the owned object that witnesses it
  +2         the five tests run again over the result, twice: over the cream, over his LUTs
  +1         run once more, over YOUR product: the handover
  +1         the gate over everything graded: show it or you can't sell it
  ) +1       closure over the content and product layers: the finish line / the identity they are for
  +1         closure that re-enters: the loop, the viewer inside it, "watch this next"
```

Every `+` is a closure over what came before, never a new peer topic. That is why nothing in the video
is left over: there are no peers, only closures.
