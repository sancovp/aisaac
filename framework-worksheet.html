Part 4

The money questions

  • Which ones are related to revenue?
  • Which ones make the most revenue?
  • Which ones hurt the most / take the most time?
  • Which ones don’t even make sense?
  • What are the levers to them (params)?
  • How do we turn this all into data?

“Doesn’t make sense” is the only answer that means delete instead of automate. Start with what makes the most money.

Also check

The health check

Eight places a business quietly loses money. Tick the ones you have measured — not the ones you think are fine.

  • Your website — how fast it loads, where visitors drop off, whether every form reaches a person
  • Your marketing — whether it brings back the customers you already won
  • Your reviews — how you ask for them, and what happens to a bad one
  • Speed to lead — how long a new enquiry waits before someone answers
  • After-hours support — what happens to a call that comes in after closing
  • Lead generation — where your next customers actually come from
  • Lead nurture — how many follow-ups a lead gets before it’s dropped
  • Content presence — what a customer finds when they look you up
