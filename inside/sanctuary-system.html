The Sanctuary System.

A myth written years before any of the code existed, whose structure the architecture later turned out to match. Kept here for the record. It is not offered as evidence of anything.

See the architecture instead →

Long before there was a runtime, there was an allegory: two characters, a transformation, and a system that produces itself. When the engine was eventually built, its parts landed in roughly the same arrangement — a personal layer, a knowledge layer, an acting layer, and a layer that rebuilds the whole thing.

That correspondence is genuinely interesting to the person it happened to. It is also exactly the kind of thing a mind will find whether or not it is there, which is why it lives on an unlisted page instead of on the front of the site.

Honest

Why this page is nearly empty.

The previous version promised a full mapping of characters to components, a section on the self-referential structure, and two books — under a "coming soon" banner, with a button to join a community that no longer exists. None of it was written.

Rather than keep a page that advertises absence, it now says the one true thing: the myth came first, the architecture rhymed with it, and the only part you can check is the architecture. If the rest ever gets written, it goes here.
