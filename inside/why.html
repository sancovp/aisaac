The claim that became a mechanism

Ethics as architecture, not compliance.

Compliance checks the work after it happens and files a report. Architecture checks the work before it acts, and refuses.

That distinction is the whole reason the runtime has a logic layer in it. Your rules — what this business will and will not do, what counts as a valid claim, what an agent is allowed to send — are written down as rules the system can evaluate, and the work is checked against them before it goes out. Not a prompt asking the model to be careful. A gate.

This is the one sentence in a large pile of philosophy that is backed by something running, which is why it is the only one carried forward to the offer.

The rest of it is not evidence.

The material this page was assembled from made much larger claims — that compassion is a competitive advantage that compounds, that a particular contemplative lineage is an engineering spec, that the value produced this way is indestructible. Those are convictions. They are why the thing was built, and they are not proof that it works.

They are kept here, unlisted, rather than deleted, because pretending the motivation was purely technical would be its own kind of dishonesty. But they stay off the front of the site, and no price anywhere is justified by them.

Two ideas worth keeping.

The business is the circulation

The instruction was not to have money — which is different from circulating it. Having none available is not the same as having none. You can have no money because it is all invested. The instruction is about flow, not poverty: keep it moving. The business is the circulation.
