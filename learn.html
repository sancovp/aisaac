Straight terms

What you’re buying, honestly.

This is not a course. There is no curriculum with a start date, no certificate, no instructor waiting for your assignment. It is a corpus of field notes plus an open-source engine, organised against a stack. If you need someone to pace you through it, that is a real need and this does not meet it yet.

It assumes you will build something. The notes were written by someone building, for people building. Read on their own they are interesting; read alongside an actual project they are useful. The whole method is that the doing comes first.

The writing is uneven, on purpose. These were published as the work happened, so some are polished and some are a working note with the dead ends still in. That is the trade for getting them at all — a tidied version would have been published a year later, or never.

No income claims. Learning this does not produce a business, and nobody can honestly promise that it will. What it produces is the ability to build the thing yourself instead of paying someone a markup to do it for you — which is the only promise here worth making.
