Straight terms

Who I’ll turn down.

Under $10K/mo, this is the wrong page for you. In the words this site used to bury behind a three-question quiz: “My retainer and automation builds are designed for businesses already doing $10K+/mo. At your stage, the ROI math doesn’t work yet — and I won’t take your money for something that won’t pay off.” What does work at that stage is rung 2, which is free, and rung 3, which is also free.

You could do all of it yourself. The engine is open source and the method is published in full. The honest reason to pay me is that while you are building the base, I am already shipping it for a living — so you spend your time on the business instead of the plumbing. That is a time argument, not a moat argument.

No income claims, anywhere. Nothing on this site promises revenue, and no figure here is a projection of what you will make. The prices are what things cost. What they earn is your side of the table.

What isn’t built yet. Rung 4 is not one-click self-serve — each one is stood up by hand on a call, which is why only a handful of slots open in a week. That is a capacity limit, not a scarcity tactic, and it is the honest reason the price is what it is.
