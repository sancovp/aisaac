How this one was made

One source. Two passes. Three surfaces.

01

What you gave it

Seven sentences of narration and a shot list. That is the whole input — no storyboard, no keyframes, no timeline scrubbed by hand.

02

What it did

Compiled two passes from the same rows: a diagram pass — layout maths, node network, spine, a live exclusion zone that keeps the diagram off the presenter — and a character pass, a rig on a transparent canvas. Layered at identical film coordinates.

03

What it produces

A rendered film, this scrubbable page, and the captions you just read. The film and the webpage are the same artifact in two moods — which is easier to show than to argue, so this page shows it.

What to expect

Forty-three seconds. That is the whole ask.

1,299 frames at thirty a second comes to about forty-three seconds end to end. Watch it in one pass, or take the clock and walk it frame by frame — it redraws at whatever rate you scrub. Nothing to install, nothing to decide, no email.

What you have at the end is an answer to exactly one question: whether there is a real engine here, or a website describing one. That is the only question this rung is built to settle, and it settles it in under a minute. Everything further down the ladder assumes you got a yes here — so if it does not hold up, stop. You are out forty-three seconds.

Honestly

What this is, and what it is not.

What this is: the engine drawing an explanation of the seven levels, live, from the same seven sentences a film renders from. That part is real and you are looking at it.

What it is not: a business running. That footage is the two empty frames above, and they stay empty until real runs fill them. A generated number in one of those frames would make every other claim on this site worthless, so there is not one.

What is also true: nobody outside the person who built this has deployed the core of it, and nobody outside him has witnessed it run. Those two rows are open on his own audit and they are restated here rather than left upstairs, because you should read everything above against them.
